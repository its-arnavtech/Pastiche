# 0014 Skip interpretation and detector defaults

- Context: spec section 5 gives the rules in words ("about 2 seconds", "soon after", "clear majority", "rare"). T-014 needs numbers, all configurable (`InterpretConfig`, `DetectorConfig` in `remixer.analytics`).
- Decision:
  - Burst: presses each within 2000 ms of the previous one. Undo: a -5 s within 6000 ms of the burst's last +5 s (or of the previous undo press) cancels the most recent uncancelled press, so overshoot keeps the intended part of the burst.
  - Late return: a later -5 s that lands in audio skipped earlier in the same listen also cancels that part (the user went back for it). Flag `late_return_cancels_skip`.
  - Window attribution: a listen counts a window as played/skipped/replayed/undone when that state covers at least 50 % of the window (`coverage_fraction`). The window is reached when at least 500 ms of it was traversed, by ear or by seek (`min_reach_ms`). A seek press attributes to the windows it jumps over, not to the window the user was in, so an anticipatory press just before a bad part does not blame the part before it.
  - Last-press credit: only the first 75 % of a burst's final net press counts as skipped (`last_press_credit = 0.75`). The user kept pressing while still in the bad part, so it ended somewhere inside the last jump. With a reaction time up to 2.5 s this never drops a truly skipped window below 50 % coverage, and it stops the landing overrun from flagging the next window. `1.0` gives the literal "N x 5 s" attribution.
  - Next: early when at or before 33 % of the track (30 s if the duration is unknown), no signal within the last 15 s or past 90 %, a half-weight negative otherwise. Previous at or before 3 s means "go to the previous track" (positive for that track); later means "restart" (positive for the current track).
  - Candidate: at least 3 listens of the track version, at least 2 skips, skip rate at least 0.6 of listens that reached the window, undo rate at most 0.2.
  - Signal weights: skip -1 per window, undo +0.25, replay +1, early next -1, mid next -0.5, previous/restart +1.
- Alternatives: span-based bursts (all presses within 2 s total; splits long bursts of fast presses); attributing a skip to the window the press happened in (blames the wrong window for anticipatory skips); total-listen denominators (spec forbids).
- Consequences: with full credit (`1.0`) the landing overrun of multi-press bursts was the only source of false positives in the simulator (precision 0.986 over 31 runs). With `0.75` it is 1.0, and recall is unchanged at 1.0. Parts skipped in only about half of the listens are not flagged ("clear majority"), so a persona that skips half the time is barely detected; that is intended. Candidates are advisory only: they feed the insights inbox and need user confirmation.

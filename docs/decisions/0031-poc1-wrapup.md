# 0031 POC 1 wrap-up: demo, product glue, resume fixes, origin isolation, release v0.1-poc1

- Context: the wrap-up brief (agent `wrapup`, T-031): freeze the stable config (decision 0030), verify POC 1 against spec section 10, build and run a small end-to-end demo, check the real-use path, list known gaps, write the POC 2 paper docs, clean the repo, release. No sustained or ramp benchmark was to be run, and none was.
- Decisions:
  - **`remixer demo` is the whole-loop smoke test** (`src/remixer/demo.py`). It runs 20 synthetic tracks of 30 s through the real stages under the stable preset in about 3 minutes (180 s on the final run): Demucs and CLAP as subprocesses of the real CLI (so the preset applies), one forced kill of the separation worker and resume, the real FastAPI backend over HTTP, a scripted listening session, the insight layer with a deterministic `MockLLM` (Ollama is opt-in with `--llm ollama`), ratings, a song ban and a section-level patch. 33 checks; any failure exits non-zero. It runs in `reports/demo/work/data`, never `data/`. `--fast` swaps the two GPU stages for ground-truth stems and stub embeddings and is what `tests/test_demo.py` runs.
  - **`src/remixer/product.py` is the missing glue** between the lanes and the planner: analysis results into the `analysis` table (song-level tempo and key, because pads and vocal lines have no pulse of their own), the player's `rate_*` events into `rating_events`, fatigue replayed from the event stream, a planner built per origin, render-and-register helpers. The backend now projects every rating it stores (`PlayerBackend.append`). Before this, nothing connected a rating made in the player to the next batch.
  - **Origins.** Every track, stem, recipe, event and rating is `synthetic` or `real`, never both. `product.py` filters by origin everywhere and raises `OriginMixError` where mixing would be silent (a recipe built from both, a taste checkpoint of the wrong kind). Rating rows carry the flag. Tests: `tests/test_real_use_path.py`.
  - **Resume bug fixed, found by the demo's forced kill.** A worker killed after finishing a unit but before its catalog write left outputs on disk; the restart saw them, acked the job as cached and never registered them, so a track silently had no stems (the embedder had the same shape). Both now register before acking (idempotent), and stale `.partial` files of dead pids are swept. `tests/test_resume.py`. A narrower window remains in the embedder (registration is not one transaction); it is in `docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.
  - **Demo planner settings.** The 20-song library cannot fill 64 slots under the 5 % per-song cap, so the demo uses 15 %, and the energy tolerance is widened because synthetic stems are flat. Real Demucs finds almost no voice in synthetic audio, so vocal stems are near-silent and the planner leaves them out of loud sections; the demo reports that instead of hiding it.
  - **Honest verdict instead of a pass.** `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md` records 4 of 5 criteria passed and 1 partial. The partial is the sustained-stress criterion: the uncapped round 1 met it, the shipped stable default does not (CPU lane below 90 %), and the tuned configuration never had a 60-minute sustain of its own.
  - **POC 2 stays on paper** (`docs/commercial.md`, `unit-economics.md`, `licenses.md`, `legal-paths.md`, `deployment.md`). The adapter boundary audit found four leaks, the main one being that stems are local files rather than `BlobStore` objects. Licenses flagged: MusicGen weights (non-commercial), Pedalboard and Rubber Band (GPL), Llama-family conditions, GeForce driver terms.
  - **Repo hygiene.** Merged branches `feature/skip-analytics`, `feature/recipe-planner` and `cursor/insight-agents-442b` were already contained in `dev`; deleting them was refused by the permission system, so it is left to the user (`docs/NEEDS_USER.md` item 9). The user's `data/` directory and `.venv-audit` were not touched. `dev` is merged into `main` with `--no-ff` and tagged `v0.1-poc1`.
- Alternatives: leave the demo on stubs (would not have found the resume bug); count the sustained-stress criterion as passed because round 1 met it (would hide that the shipped default does not); run another 60-minute sustain to close it (the brief says no more stress benchmarking).
- Consequences: `uv run remixer demo` is the first thing to run after any change to a lane; a failing check there is a regression. Real taste retraining from player ratings, real-music runs and the cooling limit remain open (`docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`).
